# How to Approach LeetCode Problems

Success on LeetCode isn't just about knowing syntax; it's about having a systematic approach.

## Step-by-Step Guide
1. **Understand the Constraints**: Look at the input size. O(n²) might pass for n=1000, but O(n log n) or O(n) is needed for n=10⁵.
2. **Brute Force First**: Think of the simplest solution. This helps you understand the problem.
3. **Identify Patterns**: Can this be solved with Two Pointers? Hashing? Sliding Window? Breadth-First Search?
4. **Dry Run**: Trace your logic with a small example or edge cases (empty list, single element, negative numbers).
5. **Optimize**: Look for redundant work in your brute force solution.
6. **Code Cleanly**: Use meaningful variable names and modularize if necessary.

## General Tips
- Don't memorize solutions. Understand the underlying pattern.
- If you're stuck for more than 45 minutes, look at a hint or the discussion tab.
